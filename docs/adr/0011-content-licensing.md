# ADR-0011: Third-party content

Status: Accepted, amended by ADR-0012 (.gp files are public), ADR-0022 (audio rendered from the transcription is stored and published with it), ADR-0024 (lyrics are stored and published) ADR-0026 (album covers are stored and published) ADR-0028 (Songsterr's bar timings for the YouTube videos are stored and published) and ADR-0029 and ADR-0030 (a recording may be fetched temporarily to measure its bar times or to transcribe its bass part)
Date: 2026-09-28

## Context

Guitar Pro transcriptions of commercial songs and song lyrics are copyrighted material. Video and audio come from YouTube and Spotify, which have their own terms.

## Decision

- Guitar Pro files are stored in the repository for personal practice and served only from an access-controlled site (ADR-0001). They are not published on a public URL.
- Lyrics are not stored in the repository. The lyrics block stays empty unless a licensed lyrics provider is integrated later. *Changed by ADR-0024 (owner, 2026-09-30): lyrics may be shared. The lyrics in the `.gp` files and in `lyrics.text` in `song.yaml` are stored and published with the song.*
- Recordings are never downloaded or re-hosted. Only official embed players are used (YouTube IFrame API, Spotify Web Playback SDK). *Changed by ADR-0029 (owner, 2026-10-11): to measure where the bars start in a recording, its audio may be fetched into a temporary folder outside the repository, with the owner's permission each time. It is deleted afterwards; only the measured times are kept. ADR-0030 (owner, 2026-10-12) does the same to transcribe a bass part where no tab exists; only the notes and the bar times are kept.*
- Count samples are recorded or generated by the owner and committed.

## Consequences

- The header's lyrics area shows nothing for now. *Since ADR-0024 the lyrics are shown in the video cell instead.*
- The hosting decision in ADR-0001 must meet the access requirement before the song files are deployed.
